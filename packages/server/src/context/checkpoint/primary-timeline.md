The session's recent entries, oldest first. The harness writes four
kinds of its own. Another model writes three of them: `[COMPACTION]`
summarizes a stretch of your conversation with the user, from the user's
messages and your final replies, and may be rewritten while that
conversation goes on; `[RESEARCH]` summarizes how a checkpoint window
changed one research file, which may since have been renamed, merged, or
removed; `[HANDOFF]` summarizes a checkpoint window that filled up
before its context turn finished. Check a detail against its source when
it matters. `[ANSWER]` records the user's answer to one of your
questions, as the user gave it. In a `[DONE]` entry, the harness
restates each entry it resolves on a `Resolves:` line.
